# Rouairwen

A small idle game made with Godot 4.7, inspired by World of Warcraft Classic. You run a Horde settlement and its guild, Lumens.

- **Settlement:** Peons walk to the gold mine, the forest and the hunting grounds, and to the Tannery, then carry what they gather back to the Great Hall. You buy gear for them (boots, picks and axes, bags) and upgrade every building from its own menu.
- **Guild:** you recruit fighters of all nine Classic classes at the Inn, in tiers from F to SS+. Each one has a spec, a role, stats, a level, gear, and spells from their class and spec.
- **Quests:** you send parties of three to Classic zones and dungeons, from the Valley of Trials to Blackrock Depths. When a quest ends, the party fights its foes turn by turn, and you can watch a replay. Victories bring back gold, cloth and gear.

## Running it

Open the `rouairwen` folder in Godot 4.7 or later and press F5.
